# Recipe conventions

Recipes in this repo are flat markdown files at the root. Gluten-free variants use a `-gf` suffix (e.g. `breakfast-cookies-gf.md`).

## Method section

**Always put each ingredient's amount in parentheses right after the ingredient's name in every Method step**, even though the amount is also in the Ingredients list. The cook should be able to read straight down the Method without flipping back to the Ingredients list.

Example:

> ### 1) Dry mix (1 min)
> Whisk the Measure for Measure (180 g), baking powder (2 tsp), salt (¾ tsp), and sugar (25 g) together. Set aside.

- Use the same amount and units as the Ingredients list (grams first, with a volume in the parentheses when the Ingredients list gives one, e.g. `(21 g / 1½ Tbsp)`).
- When a recipe has variants with different amounts, show both inline: `milk (283 g plain / 227 g banana)`.
- When an ingredient is split across steps, give the amount used *in that step*.
- Apply this to every section with a Method, including test batches and scaled versions, using that section's amounts.

## Before You Start section

**When a recipe calls for room-temp ingredients, always include an "If ingredients can't come to room temp" subsection** with:

- **Quick warm-ups** for each room-temp ingredient (e.g. whole eggs in warm tap water for 5–10 min, milk microwaved in 15-second bursts to about 100°F), with its amount in parentheses.
- **Cooking-cold adjustments** for when nothing can be warmed: ingredient swaps (e.g. oil for butter that would seize), longer mixing or rest times, and heat or time changes, each with the reason it's needed.
- Test batches or scaled sections should point to that subsection and restate any amounts that change at their scale.

This doesn't apply to recipes that intentionally call for cold ingredients (e.g. the cold butter and eggs in the breakfast cookies).
