# Breakfast Cookies with M&M's — Gluten-Free, Dense Dough-Ball Style (v2 Test Batch)

Same target as the wheat v2: **a baked dough ball** — dense, chewy all the way through, minimal spread, no crumbly/cakey give, M&M in every bite. This is **not** a straight 1:1 flour swap of the [wheat/gluten recipe](breakfast-cookies.md) — the fat, sugar, starch, *and* structure-building ingredients are rebuilt here specifically because GF blends have no gluten network to fall back on.

This is **v2**, revised after the v1 test batch came out too cakey — same root cause as the wheat version (baking powder + creamed/aerated butter), fixed the same way, **plus** one GF-specific addition to replace the chew that bread flour provides in the wheat version.

This is a **half-batch test run** (~9 cookies) so you can dial in bake time before committing to a full batch. Full-batch conversion is at the bottom.

---

## What changed from v1, and why (the actual mechanism)

The wheat v2 fix removed two lift sources (baking powder, creamed butter) and added bread flour for chew. The lift fix carries over identically here — GF flour has the same problem with baking powder and whipped-in air as wheat flour does. But bread flour's job — building a stretchy, chewy structural network — can't be replicated with a GF flour, because that network is gluten. The GF equivalent tool for that specific job is **psyllium husk powder**: when it hydrates, it forms a gel with genuine viscoelastic stretch, which is the standard way GF bread bakers get a "bready" chew instead of a sandy/crumbly one. It's doing structurally the same job bread flour does in the wheat version, just via a gel network instead of a gluten network.

- **Baking powder — cut entirely.** Same reasoning as wheat v2: zero lift wanted.
- **Baking soda — cut way down (⅓ tsp → ⅛ tsp).** Browning/chew only now, not spread/lift.
- **Cold, cubed butter instead of softened, creamed butter.** Mix on low just until the butter breaks into pea-sized bits coated in sugar — sandy/crumbly, **not** light and fluffy. No whipped-in air, no cake structure for the oven to expand.
- **Psyllium husk powder added (1 tsp).** This is the new ingredient doing the "bready" job. It hydrates during the chill step (using moisture already in the dough) and forms a gel that gives the crumb genuine chew and hold instead of the sandy/crumbly texture GF cookies default to.
- **Cornstarch trimmed slightly (1 tsp → ¾ tsp).** Now that psyllium is providing structure, stacking a full dose of tenderizing cornstarch on top works against the chew you're building.
- **Sugar trimmed slightly, brown-sugar-heavy.** Less total sugar = less spread and less of the shiny, crackly, thin-cookie surface.
- **Butter bumped slightly (127 g → 135 g).** The cold-mix method and the psyllium (which draws in moisture as it hydrates) both benefit from a touch more fat so the crumb doesn't dry out.
- **Egg + yolk stay cold**, not room temp — same logic as the wheat version: keeps the cold butter from softening/aerating prematurely during mixing.
- **Hotter, longer bake, no early pull.** You confirmed you want the center **fully set**, not gooey. This bakes a bit hotter and longer than v1 GF (which was tuned to pull early and stay underdone) — the dense, low-lift, psyllium-structured dough can handle the extra time without drying out the way a v1-style GF dough would.

## Before You Start
- **Butter:** straight from the fridge, **cold**. Cut into ½" cubes right before mixing — don't let it soften. Cold butter is the point now: it's what keeps the dough from aerating when mixed, same as the wheat v2.
- **Egg + egg yolk:** also straight from the fridge, **cold** — don't bring to room temp this time. A cold egg dropped into the cold butter/sugar mix keeps everything from prematurely softening.
- **Everything else** (GF blend, psyllium, sugars, baking soda, cornstarch, salt, vanilla, xanthan gum, M&M's) is temperature-neutral — pull straight from the pantry, no prep needed.
- The dough goes **colder still** once mixed — see the chill step below. It's doing double duty here: firming the cold butter chunks back up, *and* giving the psyllium time to hydrate and the GF starches time to fully absorb moisture.

## Ingredients — Test Batch (~9 cookies)

### Dry
- 160 g gluten-free 1:1 baking blend (weigh it — don't use a cup measure)
- 1 tsp psyllium husk powder
- ¾ tsp cornstarch
- ⅛ tsp baking soda
- ⅜ tsp fine sea salt
- ½ tsp xanthan gum — **only if your blend doesn't already include it**

### Wet
- 135 g organic unsalted butter, **cold, cut into ½" cubes** (not softened)
- 105 g organic light brown sugar, packed
- 20 g organic cane sugar
- 1 large organic egg (about 50 g) + 1 organic egg yolk (about 18 g), cold
- 2 tsp organic vanilla extract

### Mix-in
- 128 g mini M&M's (or 120 g regular, roughly chopped)

---

## Method

### 1) Dry mix (2 min)
Whisk GF blend, psyllium husk powder, cornstarch, baking soda, salt (and xanthan gum, if using) together. Set aside.

### 2) Cut cold butter into the sugars (1–2 min)
Add the cold cubed butter, brown sugar, and cane sugar to the mixer bowl. Mix on **low** just until the butter breaks down into pea-sized (or smaller) bits coated in sugar — the mixture should look **sandy/crumbly, not light or fluffy**. If it starts looking smooth and pale, stop immediately — that's the creaming action that made v1 cakey.

### 3) Egg + yolk + vanilla (20–30 sec)
Add the cold egg, egg yolk, and vanilla. Mix on **low** just until the dough starts to come together into a shaggy mass.

### 4) Add dry mix (20–30 sec)
Add the dry mix in one go. Mix on **low** just until no dry streaks remain and the dough looks cohesive but shaggy — not smooth. GF dough will look slightly softer/tackier than wheat dough at this stage; that's expected, not a sign to add more flour.

### 5) Fold in M&M's (30 sec)
Fold in the M&M's by hand — mixed into the dough, not pressed on after.

### 6) Scoop, then chill (60–90 min minimum, up to 24 hr)
Scoop into ~46 g mounds (a 2 Tbsp / #20 scoop gets you close) onto parchment — about 13 tall mounds total. **Do not flatten them.** Sizing down from the original 3 Tbsp scoop is intentional — a smaller mound has less distance for heat to travel to the center, which helps it finish baking through in the same window instead of setting on the outside before the middle catches up.

Cover and chill **at least 60–90 minutes**. This is doing three jobs at once now: keeping the cold butter chunks solid, letting the GF starches finish hydrating, and giving the psyllium time to gel and build its chew. Don't shortcut this — it's the step doing the most work in this recipe.

> If short on time, 45–60 min in the freezer is the minimum acceptable substitute — don't go shorter with this dough.

### 7) Bake (15–18 min)
- Oven: **380°F (193°C)**, rack in the middle.
- Use a **cool/room-temp baking sheet**.
- Space mounds 2–3" apart.
- Bake **15–18 minutes** — rotate the pan halfway. Like the wheat version, this runs longer than mound size alone would suggest: the cold cubed butter, cold egg, and the 60–90 min chill on top of that mean the dough starts from a noticeably colder point than a typical dough, so part of the bake time goes into bringing the center up to temperature before it can finish setting. (The wheat version's window was tested and needed to go up from 13–16 to 16–19 min to reliably hit temp without a rescue bake — the GF window here is bumped proportionally.)
- **Doneness check — use a thermometer, not just looks:** starting at the low end of the range, pull one cookie and probe the center. Target **195–200°F** (pull at the lower end of that vs. the wheat version's 200–205°F, since GF crumb dries out faster once fully set) — confirmed against an actual bite on the wheat version: at temp, the crumb should be soft, moist, and chewy but hold together, not smear or taste raw. A matte, no-jiggle surface is a useful secondary check, but the surface can look set before the center actually is — check with the thermometer before trusting looks alone.

### 8) Cool on the sheet (10 min)
Let them sit on the hot sheet for **10 minutes**, then transfer to a rack.

## Test-batch tasting checklist
- **Still cakey / open crumb** → check step 2: did the butter-sugar mixture stay sandy, or did it creep toward pale/fluffy? Chill the butter cubes 10 min in the freezer right before mixing if your kitchen is warm.
- **Sandy/crumbly, not chewy** → the psyllium may not have fully hydrated — confirm you chilled the full 60–90 min. If it's still short on chew after that, try bumping psyllium to 1¼ tsp next batch (go slow — too much reads as gummy).
- **Gummy or slimy texture** → you've overdone psyllium and/or xanthan. Cut psyllium back to ¾ tsp, and double check your GF blend didn't already have xanthan before you added more.
- **Spread too flat** → chill longer, confirm butter was truly cold (not just cool) going in, verify oven temp with a thermometer.
- **Dry** → bump butter up 10 g next time, or add 1 Tbsp organic whole milk with the wet ingredients.
- **Soft in the middle — know which kind you have.** Soft **and holding together** (tender, moist, chewy, tears rather than smears, doesn't taste raw) is the *target texture* — that's correct, not underbaked. Soft **and wet** (smears, tastes like raw dough, doesn't hold shape) means it's genuinely underbaked, not gummy from psyllium — don't just add a minute and guess again, probe with a thermometer next time (target 195–200°F) so you know exactly how far off you were, then extend the time by that gap. This is a cold-dough issue, not a recipe-ratio issue.
- **Good — soft-but-holds-together chew, fully set, M&M in every bite** → note your exact bake time and internal temp that worked in *your* oven and use it for the full batch.

## Full-batch conversion (once the test batch is dialed in)
Multiply everything by **2.75×** for ~34–36 cookies (smaller 2 Tbsp scoop; weigh everything — don't scale by cup measure):
- 440 g gluten-free 1:1 baking blend
- 2¾ tsp psyllium husk powder
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- ⅓ tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp fine sea salt
- 1⅓ tsp xanthan gum, only if your blend doesn't already include it
- 371 g butter, cold, cubed
- 289 g brown sugar
- 55 g cane sugar
- 2 large eggs (about 100 g) + 2 egg yolks (about 36 g), cold
- 1 Tbsp + 2½ tsp vanilla extract
- 352 g mini M&M's

Same method, same (or longer) 60–90 min chill — bake in batches so the dough balls stay cold going in.
